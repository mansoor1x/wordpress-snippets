# BigDigit Consultants Website

A one-page marketing site for **BigDigit Consultants**, a bookkeeping, payroll management and tax preparation firm. The layout follows Jegtheme's FSE accounting templates (Acctop).

## Sections
Top bar · sticky header · hero with dashboard mock-up · feature strip · about · services (bookkeeping, payroll, tax preparation, tax planning, compliance, advisory) · stats · process · why choose us · federal filing calendar · pricing · price estimator · testimonials · FAQ · contact form · footer.

## Files
- `index.html`: page markup
- `assets/styles.css`: styles, including the responsive breakpoints
- `assets/script.js`: mobile menu, scroll reveal, count-up stats and active nav link
- `assets/tools.js`: filing-calendar tabs and next-deadline highlight, and the price estimator (rates are in `RATES` at the top)
- `assets/favicon.svg`: logo mark
