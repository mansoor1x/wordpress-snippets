Federal Filing Calendar

Never miss a federal filing deadline

The key IRS due dates for sole proprietors, S-corps and partnerships, and employers, in one place. We track them for our clients.

Sole Prop / 1040

For sole proprietors, freelancers and individuals filing Form 1040 with Schedule C.

  1. Jan15

    Q4 estimated tax payment

    • Form 1040-ES payment for the last quarter of the prior tax year.
  2. Apr15

    File your return or request an extension

    • File Form 1040 with Schedule C and Schedule SE, or file Form 4868 for an automatic 6-month extension.
    • Pay any tax owed. An extension gives more time to file, not more time to pay.
    • Q1 estimated tax payment (Form 1040-ES).
    • Last day for prior-year IRA and HSA contributions.
  3. Jun15

    Q2 estimated tax payment

    • Form 1040-ES payment covering April and May.
  4. Sep15

    Q3 estimated tax payment

    • Form 1040-ES payment covering June through August.
  5. Oct15

    Extended return deadline

    • Last day to file Form 1040 if you filed Form 4868.

S-Corp / Partnership

For S corporations (Form 1120-S) and partnerships or multi-member LLCs (Form 1065).

  1. Jan15

    Owner Q4 estimated tax payment

    • Owners pay Form 1040-ES for the last quarter of the prior year on their share of profit.
  2. Mar15

    Business returns and K-1s

    • File Form 1120-S or Form 1065 and send Schedule K-1s to shareholders and partners.
    • Or file Form 7004 for an automatic 6-month extension.
    • Last day to file Form 2553 for an S election effective this year.
  3. Apr15

    Owners' personal returns

    • Owners file Form 1040 with their K-1 income, or file Form 4868 to extend.
    • Owner Q1 estimated tax payment (Form 1040-ES).
  4. Jun15

    Owner Q2 estimated tax payment

    • Form 1040-ES payment covering April and May.
  5. Sep15

    Extended business returns

    • Extended Form 1120-S and Form 1065 are due.
    • Owner Q3 estimated tax payment (Form 1040-ES).
  6. Oct15

    Extended owner returns

    • Last day to file Form 1040 if owners filed Form 4868.

Employers

For businesses with employees or contractors: payroll tax returns, W-2s and 1099s.

  1. Monthly15th

    Payroll tax deposits

    • Monthly depositors deposit withheld income tax and FICA by the 15th of the following month.
    • Semiweekly depositors follow the Wednesday and Friday schedule. FUTA is deposited when the liability passes $500.
  2. Jan31

    Year-end payroll and contractor filings

    • Send Form W-2 to employees and file W-2 and W-3 with the SSA.
    • Send Form 1099-NEC to contractors and file it with the IRS.
    • File Form 941 for Q4 and Form 940 (FUTA) for the year.
  3. Apr30

    Form 941 for Q1

    • Quarterly return for January through March.
  4. Jul31

    Form 941 for Q2

    • Quarterly return for April through June.
  5. Oct31

    Form 941 for Q3

    • Quarterly return for July through September.

Dates are the standard federal due dates for calendar-year filers. When a date falls on a weekend or federal holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day. State and local deadlines differ, and special cases such as fiscal years or disaster relief can change yours. This calendar is general guidance, not tax advice. Contact us to confirm your dates.
