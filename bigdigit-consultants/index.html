Ask a Question →
Which businesses do you work with?

We serve small and mid-sized businesses across many industries, including retail, restaurants, construction, professional services, e-commerce and startups, as well as individuals and families.

Which accounting software do you use?

We work with leading cloud platforms such as QuickBooks Online and Xero, and can help you choose and set up the right tool if you don't have one yet.

Can you catch up on books that are behind?

Yes. Catch-up and clean-up bookkeeping is one of our specialties. We'll bring your records current and get you back on a regular monthly schedule.

How does payroll onboarding work?

We gather your employee and tax information, set up the payroll system, verify everything with a test run, and then take over each payroll cycle and its filings.

Do you prepare individual tax returns?

Yes. We prepare individual, business, partnership and corporate tax returns, plus quarterly estimated payments and year-round tax planning.
